Accessibility · 2 October 2026

Everyone should be able to check before they book.

If any part of this site gets in your way, that is our problem to fix, not yours to work around.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA across this site, and we treat accessibility barriers as bugs to fix, not features to explain.

What is built in

Visible keyboard focus on every link and button.

Animation turns off when your device asks for reduced motion.

Text that resizes on phones, with tap targets at least 44 pixels tall.

No accounts, logins or time limits to get through.

Where we know it falls short

Checkout runs on Stripe and booking runs on Cal.com. Those pages are built by those companies, and we can't fix them directly.

Some downloadable guides may not yet be fully tagged for screen readers. If you need one in another format, ask and we will send it.

Tell us about a barrier

If anything on this site is hard to use with your device, screen reader or settings, email justask@beforeyoubookthatsurgery.com with the page and what happened. We reply within five business days, and we will get you the information in a format that works for you while we fix it.

Last reviewed
