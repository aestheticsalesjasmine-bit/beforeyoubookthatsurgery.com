Independence & privacy · Version 1.1 · 27 September 2026

Your answers stay with you.

There are no accounts. What you enter in the Light Map and My Path is saved in your own browser, and we can't see it. We count visits without cookies, and we only learn more if you choose to share, anonymously.

01 · Stays on your device

Everything you type

Light Map answers and your decision note. My Path: health baseline, deposit questions, kit, recovery log, reflections and vision board. The file you download is yours to keep or delete.

02 · Counted anonymously

Totals, never people

Page visits, country, device type and the site you came from, through Plausible: no cookies, no stored IP address, hosted in the EU. Your "Did this help you pause?" answer, if you give one.

03 · Only if you choose

Share my path anonymously

Sent once: your procedure type, whether you're traveling, the stage you've reached, and which deposit questions weren't answered in writing. Never your name, email, notes, health answers or reflections. It can't be traced back to you, so it can't be unsent.

The Independence Pledge

Paid by you. Never by surgeons.

What we never take

— Referral fees or commissions from surgeons, clinics, facilitators, recovery houses or lenders.— Affiliate income. We don’t sell recovery kits, garments or supplements.— Sponsorships of our content, events or community.— Free or discounted procedures, for anyone on our team.

How we make money

Today the site, the Light Map, My Path and the Permission Call are free. Any paid service we add is paid by you, priced per engagement, and never depends on whether you go ahead. Choosing not to have surgery is a good outcome here.

Each January we publish a statement of where every dollar came from.

What we do, and never do

We help you find what can be checked, what still needs asking, and what you want. We don’t diagnose, assess whether you’re a candidate, or recommend procedures, surgeons or clinics. That belongs to licensed clinicians.

Stories, not testimonials

We don’t publish patient testimonials or before-and-after photos. When we share a story, it’s about the decision process, with consent and identifying details removed, and it’s marked as such.

If we get it wrong

Tell us at justask@beforeyoubookthatsurgery.com. Corrections are made in public, and earlier versions stay on the record.

What you send us directly

Booking a Permission Call through Cal.com shares your name, email and chosen time. Please don't put health details in the booking form; we'll talk on the call.

Emailing justask@beforeyoubookthatsurgery.com shares whatever you write. Please don't send medical records or health details by email.

We use these only to hold the call and reply to you.

What we never do

— Sell or rent any information. — Use advertising pixels, tracking cookies or session recording. — Share anything with surgeons, practices, facilities or financiers. — Ask you to create an account.

Who handles it, and where

Cloudflare hosts the site and routes our email (United States and global network). Plausible Analytics counts visits (European Union). Cal.com handles bookings (United States). Google hosts our inbox (United States). If you're in Australia or Canada, information you send us directly may be stored outside your country with these providers.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We'll reply within 30 days. This includes rights under US state privacy laws, Australia's Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles, and Canada's PIPEDA and provincial laws, including Québec's Law 25.

If you're not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to your regulator: in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial commissioner.

Consumer health data privacy policy

What counts as health data here, and your rights over it.

What it covers

Anything you enter about your body, health, procedures or recovery is consumer health data. It stays in your own browser and we never receive it.

The only health-related information that can reach us is the optional anonymous share: procedure type, travel, stage, Light Map reading and which deposit questions weren’t answered in writing. It has no name, email or IP address attached.

Why we collect it

Only to publish pooled patterns that help the next patient, once 100 paths are shared. We ask for your consent before anything is sent, every time.

Who it’s shared with

Nobody, except the processors that run the site (listed above). We never sell it, and never share it with surgeons, clinics, facilitators, lenders or advertisers.

Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what we hold, give you a copy, delete it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email justask@beforeyoubookthatsurgery.com. We reply within 30 days, and you can appeal a decision by replying to that email.

This policy is written for Washington’s My Health My Data Act and Nevada’s consumer health data law, and applies to everyone. Australian visitors are also covered by the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.
